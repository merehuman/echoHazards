echoHazards
How it works

echoHazards combines federal environmental records from the NRC (spills), EPA ECHO (violations), and EPA TRI (chemical releases) into one searchable map. Enter an address or click the map, set a radius, and filter by source or medium to see nearby incidents. Click a marker for details and links to government reports where available.
